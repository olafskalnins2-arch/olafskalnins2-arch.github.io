Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy covers homedecor pin scheduler ("the app"), a personal tool that prepares home decor pins and publishes them to the operator's own Pinterest business account after the operator approves each one. The app is operated by an individual based in Latvia (European Union).

Who uses the app

The app is for personal use only. It connects to a single Pinterest account, the operator's own. No other Pinterest users can sign in to it or are affected by it.

What data the app accesses

  • Pinterest access tokens for the operator's own account, obtained through Pinterest's official OAuth sign-in.
  • The operator's own boards and pins, read to choose the board to post to (scopes boards:read and pins:read).
  • Pins the operator approves, created on the operator's own board (scope pins:write).

The app does not access, collect or store any data about other Pinterest users. That includes their profiles, pins, boards, followers and messages.

How data is stored

Access tokens are kept as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository and used only to call the Pinterest API. A log of published pins (date, pin ID and product link) is kept in that private repository. Draft pins are sent to a private Discord channel so the operator can approve or skip them.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. It is used only to publish the operator's own pins.

Affiliate links

Published pins may link to retailers through affiliate programs and are labelled #ad. When someone clicks such a link, the retailer or affiliate network may use cookies under its own privacy policy. The app itself does not track visitors.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the app is in use. Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest's settings under Security & logins → Apps, which stops the app from working. Stored tokens and logs are deleted when the app is retired.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can ask what personal data is held about you and request that it be corrected or deleted. You also have the right to complain to Latvia's Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija).
